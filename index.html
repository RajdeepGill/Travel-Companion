    🚕 Getting Around

    Walking the Strip
    Most of the plan is walkable, but distances are longer than they look — one "block" between resorts can be 10–15 minutes. Use the pedestrian bridges at major intersections.
    Rideshare (Uber / Lyft)Airport ↔ hotels, Luxor → LINQ with bags, Welcome sign
    Free tram: Mandalay Bay – Luxor – ExcaliburHandy on the south end
    Las Vegas MonorailPaid • Harrah's/The LINQ station is right by the hotel • MGM Grand ↔ Sahara
    The Deuce (RTC bus)Paid • runs 24 hours along the Strip
    At LAS, follow the Ride Share signs — pickup is in the parking garage, not at the curb.
